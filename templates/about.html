THE CORE IDEA

Same terrain. Different observation.

Lunar imagery may differ because of viewpoint, scale, illumination, processing and acquisition conditions. LUNARMATCH attempts to determine whether two observations contain a geometrically consistent set of corresponding visual structures.

Instead of treating one similarity number as a final answer, V2 separates correspondence, geometric verification, metadata validation and reference evidence.

LOCALIZATION

Coordinates are evidence, not decoration.

If an image contains valid latitude/longitude metadata, or a legitimate reference source supplies calibrated coordinates, LUNARMATCH can expose that information in the validation layer.

If coordinates are unavailable, the platform explicitly reports that limitation rather than generating or guessing a location.

RESPONSIBLE COMPUTATION

A correspondence is not automatically a discovery.

LUNARMATCH is designed to expose the evidence behind its output. A strong computational correspondence can support further investigation, but mission identity, coordinates and ground truth require independent supporting evidence.
