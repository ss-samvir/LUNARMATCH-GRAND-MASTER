WHY LUNARMATCH

From visual similarity to defensible evidence.

Comparing planetary imagery is not only about finding visual similarity. A useful system must also communicate how a correspondence was obtained, what evidence supports it and where the available data ends.

LUNARMATCH follows that principle by separating computational correspondence from ground-truth validation and by refusing to invent unavailable metadata.
