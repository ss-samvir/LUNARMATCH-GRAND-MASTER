CURRENT V2 ENGINE

Classical vision. Deterministic evidence.

The current correspondence engine is based on established computer-vision methods rather than a trained black-box probability model.

The resulting correspondence/reliability indicator should therefore be interpreted as computational evidence from the pipeline, not as a trained-model probability or independently established ground-truth accuracy.

PLANETARY DATA / INTEROPERABILITY

Preserve the evidence. Never guess it.

Planetary and geospatial imagery can carry information about coordinate reference systems, projections, datums, acquisition conditions, mission identity and instruments.

LUNARMATCH is designed so such fields can be preserved and surfaced when genuinely supplied by the source. Missing information remains explicitly unavailable.
