HOW TO READ THE REPORT

Four evidence layers.

No single metric should be treated as proof of geographic truth. The report separates computational matching from geometric and scientific validation.

01

FEATURES

Measures whether distinctive structures could be detected in the supplied images.

02

MATCHES

Measures candidate descriptor correspondences after matching and consistency filtering.

03

GEOMETRY

Measures whether matched points support a consistent geometric transformation.

04

VALIDATION

Reports independent metadata and reference evidence separately from the visual match.

```
```
!
SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION

A computational match does not automatically establish location.

Coordinates, mission identity and instrument information are shown only when supported by available metadata or legitimate reference/calibration evidence. Otherwise the report explicitly marks the information as unavailable or unestablished.
