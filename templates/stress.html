WHY STRESS TEST?

A correspondence system should be tested beyond the ideal case.

Real lunar imagery can contain changes in illumination, noise, blur, scale and other acquisition differences. Controlled perturbation testing helps identify where correspondence evidence remains stable and where it becomes uncertain.

Scientific interpretation

Stress testing with synthetic perturbations is not equivalent to validation on independent lunar mission imagery. It is a controlled robustness experiment and should be presented as such.

For stronger Round-2 evaluation, repeat each perturbation at multiple strengths and, where possible, evaluate against independent reference image pairs with known correspondence.
