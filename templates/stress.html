WHY STRESS TEST?

Reliability under adversarial conditions.

A high correspondence score on one clean image pair is not sufficient evidence of robustness. Stress tests deliberately alter image conditions and observe whether the correspondence engine remains geometrically consistent.

ROBUSTNESS ≠ ACCURACY

Passing a stress test demonstrates behavior under a particular controlled transformation. It does not establish geographic ground truth.

02

FAILURE IS DATA

A degraded correspondence result is retained as an observable limitation rather than artificially converted into a successful match.

03

REPRODUCIBLE TESTS

Each condition has a defined transformation and severity so that the same experiment can be repeated during judging or further development.
