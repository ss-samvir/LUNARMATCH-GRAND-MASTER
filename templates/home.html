WHY LUNARMATCH

A match is not automatically a location.

Conventional image matching can indicate that two images contain visually corresponding structures. That does not, by itself, prove where an observation was acquired.

LUNARMATCH therefore treats localization as an evidence problem. Coordinates are displayed only when they are genuinely supported by available metadata or reference information.
