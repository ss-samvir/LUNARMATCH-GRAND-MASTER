WHY LUNARMATCH

Make visual correspondence inspectable.

Lunar imagery can contain repeated geological structures, different illumination conditions, scale changes and imaging artifacts. A useful correspondence system should therefore expose its evidence rather than simply return a single unexplained number.

LUNARMATCH combines computer-vision correspondence, geometric verification and metadata-aware validation into one accessible research interface.
